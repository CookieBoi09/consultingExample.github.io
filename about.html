About Northstar

/ 01

Machine learning should create momentum.

Northstar ML is an independent consultancy for teams that want practical progress—not another slide deck, black-box prototype, or science project.

Our point of view

The best ML work lives at the intersection of commercial judgment, rigorous engineering, and a genuine understanding of the people who will use it.

We partner with leaders and technical teams to navigate that intersection. Sometimes that means sharpening the strategy before a line of code is written. Sometimes it means joining an existing team to get a critical system over the line. In every case, the goal is the same: durable value and stronger internal capability.

Working principles

How we show up.

Small-team attention, senior-level judgment, and an honest view of what the technology can—and cannot—do.
